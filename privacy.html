Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Studio publishing is a private tool that one person runs on their own computer, for their own accounts only. This page explains what it reads, where that is kept, and how to remove it.

What it accesses

  • YouTube: this app uses YouTube API Services. It uploads videos to the owner's channel, and reads the channel's videos and their analytics, including revenue figures.
  • Instagram: it publishes posts to the owner's professional account, and reads that account's basic profile, posts and insights.
  • Facebook: it lists the Pages the owner manages, publishes posts to the owner's Page, and reads that Page's posts, engagement, insights and estimated earnings figures where Facebook provides them.

It only acts on accounts the owner has signed in with, and it does not sign in to anyone else's accounts. Once a week it also searches YouTube for public videos on topics the owner chooses, and reads their public titles, view counts and thumbnails, together with the public statistics of the channels that posted them.

To publish to Instagram, a video is made available at a temporary public web address for the length of the upload, a few minutes and never more than twenty, while Instagram collects it.

How the information is used and kept

  • The sign-in keys are stored encrypted on the owner's computer.
  • Video details and analytics figures are stored in a database on the owner's computer. They are used to post videos and to show the owner how each video and channel is doing. The titles and descriptions of videos already on the owner's accounts are used to avoid repeating topics.
  • To plan videos and avoid repeats, the titles, topics and opening lines of descriptions of existing videos, and the public titles, view counts and thumbnails the weekly search finds, are sent to an AI model through OpenRouter, which passes them to the model provider the owner has chosen. The owner may also let an AI coding assistant read the stored figures and estimated earnings on their own computer. Those AI services are governed by their own terms.
  • Apart from the AI services described above, information from the owner's accounts is not sold, not used for advertising and not given to anyone else.

Removing access and data

  • YouTube: remove access at Google's security settings page.
  • Instagram and Facebook: remove the app in the account's settings (under Apps and websites, or Business integrations).
  • Removing access at Google, Instagram or Facebook stops all further reading, but does not delete data already stored. Stored figures, video details and keys stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them: the studio's disconnect command removes a channel's keys, and deleting the studio's data folder removes everything else.
  • To ask about any of this, open an issue at this page's repository.

Terms that also apply

Using the YouTube features means you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
