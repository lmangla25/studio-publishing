Privacy policy

Studio publishing is a private tool, run on its owner's computer for the owner's own accounts only.

What it does

It posts the owner's videos to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts, and reads how they perform. This app uses YouTube API Services. It also reads public YouTube video details to research topics.

Where the information goes

Everything is stored on the owner's computer, with sign-in keys encrypted. Video titles and topics are sent to an AI model service to plan videos. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Removing access

Remove access to YouTube at Google's security settings, and to Instagram or Facebook in the account's settings. Stored data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it.

See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
